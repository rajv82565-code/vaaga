(01) The Arts Day of CETP

About VAAGA

Once a year, the engineers and architects of CETP put their lab records and drafting sheets away and pick up chilankas, microphones and paintbrushes. VAAGA is a celebration of every beat, brushstroke and verse our campus has been saving for this stage.

Tradition Reinvention

Version 26.2.0 goes further. It draws on the Theyyam heartland we call home and brings those roots together with the sound of a new generation, so tradition and reinvention share one spotlight.

Moments from VAAGA

01 / 06

Arts Day, through the lens

The lights go up

Opening night on the main stage. The rig comes alive, the first voice finds the mic, and a campus forgets it has classes tomorrow.

Own the floor

Mundus hitched, shades on, sequins catching every light. The dance crews didn't just perform on the floor, they took it.

Sing it back

A duet that turned the auditorium into a choir. Two voices on stage and a few hundred more singing along from the seats.

Colour everywhere

Crimson on the ramp, red satin under the lasers. Arts Day has never done quiet colours.

Every corner a stage

A classical solo under the main rig, a jersey-clad crew in the courtyard. Some of the best moments never needed a spotlight.

Fire and roots

The Theyyam tribute that closed the night, then the honours. Crimson, flame and drums, and a stage that remembered where it all began.
